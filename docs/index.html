Conflict Risk Monitor

Which countries are most likely to see armed-conflict violence in the next three months? Estimates come from a model trained on 35+ years of UCDP conflict-event data and World Bank indicators.

Read this first: these are statistical estimates for research and learning, not predictions of war or advice. The model only knows past violence and a few development indicators; it cannot see diplomacy, elections or sudden shocks.
